Production Specifications

Visual Style

  • Clean office, dispatcher, and homeowner phone/form visuals
  • No robot imagery, no futuristic AI graphics, no cheesy stock
  • Avoid over-produced "tech commercial" feel — target business operations tone
  • Simple text overlays: white or light text on semi-transparent blue panel
  • Cuts should feel deliberate — 3–6 shots total, not rapid-fire
  • Full color for resolved/solution scenes; slightly desaturated for problem scenes

Voiceover Direction

  • Male or female — calm, measured, business credibility (not announcer voice)
  • Pace: ~135 words per 40 seconds — do not rush
  • No dramatic pauses, no hypey inflection on product name
  • Natural breath between "We start with…" and the audit name
  • Final two sentences slightly slower — let the CTA land

Music / Audio

  • Light, understated background track — corporate/neutral, not inspirational
  • No dramatic swells; music should sit ~10–15 dB below VO
  • Subtle fade out under end card
  • No sound effects on text animations

Text Overlay Treatment

  • Font: Inter, Montserrat, or similar clean sans-serif
  • Weight: 600–700 for headlines; 400 for supporting copy
  • Background: semi-transparent blue panel (rgba 1E4A8C / 88%)
  • Fade in: ~12 frames; hold with VO beat; fade out: ~8 frames
  • Position: lower-third or center depending on visual composition
  • Scene 5 bullets: plain white text on gray/dark overlay — no panels

Export / Deliverables

  • Primary: 1920×1080 H.264, ~10–15 Mbps, .mp4
  • Social cut: 1080×1080 square version (optional)
  • Captions: SRT file for accessibility / LinkedIn autoplay
  • End card: editable PSD or AE comp so contact info can be updated

Brand Colors

  • Primary blue: #1E4A8C
  • Active/link blue: #2D6EC7
  • Accent teal: #0FA3B1
  • Body gray: #4A5568
  • Background: #F7F9FC / White
